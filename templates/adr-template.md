> An **ADR** (Architecture / Any Decision Record) captures **one** significant decision and
> the reasoning behind it, so the choice can be revisited *deliberately* rather than
> drifting silently. Keep it short. See [`docs/decisions/README.md`](../decisions/README.md).
>
> _**Note on links:** the relative paths in this template are written relative to its
> **destination** (`docs/decisions/`, where ADRs live). They resolve once this file is copied
> there — not from `templates/`._
